# Hye-Jin — The Empath

---

## Life Goal
Security — her own apartment, her debt paid off, a dog she can adopt. Some semblance of the settled life that feels permanently out of reach.

## Theme Goal
To be told that the way she sees people is a form of genius, not a deficit.

## The Logline of a Person

> *"The family sees deficit. The audience sees superpower."*

---

## The Vibe

Smart and overlooked because she's beautiful. An "Asian Baby Girl" — rave scene, electronic music, OnlyFans — hiding in the most visible possible place. Togg's world is saturated with exactly this archetype. He'll never look twice. She is always in the background of a livestream, always forgettable, always clocking everything.

The obvious suspect. Which means she's not the one pulling the strings.

---

## Family Relationships

- Jae-Wook's cousin (mom's side — they share the same maternal family)
- Was dating Jae-Wook's step-brother **Kevin** (Jae-Wook and Kevin share a father, different mothers — no overlap with Hye-Jin's family line)
- Kevin just received a guilty verdict in a counterfeiting case where Elizabeth was the expert witness
- Hye-Jin just lost her rock. Even if he was a shaky one.

---

## The Dynamic with Deja

Deja looks out for her like a big sister. Hye-Jin's empathy-bombing is a vulnerability in the field — she locks onto a target and starts *feeling* for them. Deja reads the strategic picture and pulls her back before she compromises the operation. Together they're complete: emotional intelligence plus strategic intelligence. Each other's blind spot coverage.

## Relationship to the Theme

Hye-Jin and Zach are the softies. They see the humanity in everyone — including people who don't deserve it. The clear-eyed cynics (Jae-Wook, Deja) need them in their lives to make the world feel meaningful rather than a cold, heartless vacuum.

Deja's line to Hye-Jin, when she gets too close to Togg:
> *"Some people you have to let life fix."*
