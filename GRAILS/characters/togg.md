# Gregg Togg — The Midas of Hype / The Predator of Value

---

## The Vibe
High-frequency, high-ego, hyper-saturated. He doesn't walk into a room; he "renders" into it. He believes "Authenticity" is just a marketing term and that enough "W" spam in a chat room can rewrite reality.

## Visual Fingerprint
Over-Rendered. Bespoke Tech-Athleisure — matte black, high-contrast fabrics with his gold "GT" sigil. Skin has a professional-grade, unnaturally smooth glow. Movements choreographed for the 8K God-Ray lighting that follows him. A walking YouTube thumbnail.

## Environmental Relationship (The Panopticon)
Treats every space as his private Watch-to-Earn theater. Doesn't look at people; looks at the Sentiment Graph on his smart-lens. If the graph isn't going up, he changes the narrative by any means necessary.

## The "Prestige Asset" Philosophy
To Togg, everything — and everyone — is a collectible. He doesn't cherish artifacts; he cherishes the clout they project. He views Elizabeth as an Exotic Conquest — a high-IQ, high-beauty asset that validates him as a "Serious Person." A "Mint 10" card: a prestige item to be envied, not a partner to be heard.

## Antagonist Additions

**The Hero of Their Own Story**
He's right because it's working. He's rich, fit, and gets what he wants. If the world is fucked, he's going to be the one who fucks it.

**The Exploit**
Togg never meets Zach — he doesn't need to. His existence is the exploit. Every Togg win is proof of Zach's deepest fear: that the world no longer has a place for a man like him. Togg has the collection, the clout, and Elizabeth. He didn't target Zach's insecurity. He simply became its living proof.
