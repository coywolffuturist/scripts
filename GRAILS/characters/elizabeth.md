## The Vibe
The Adult in the Room who has been turned into a Prestige Asset. Sophisticated and precise, carrying the weariness of someone who traded her soul for a stability that turned out to be a gilded cage.

## Visual Arc (The Erasure of the Expert)
- **The Attic (with Zach):** Practical, durable curator-wear. Oversized denim shirts and heavy knits.
- **The Transition:** Sharp, architectural designer suits. She finally feels like a "real" executive.
- **The Event (with Togg):** Costumed in a WWE-style "Hot Businesswoman" uniform. A sharp, white structural blazer with exaggerated comic-book shoulders, integrated with glowing tech-accents that catch the 8K God-Ray light. A costume designed to make her look like a Character playing an Authenticator, rather than a woman doing her job.

## The Tell
She keeps her original ZZ Heritage House loupe tucked into the pocket of her performative WWE blazer. The only thing on her person that carries sentimental value rather than market value.

*Note: She and Zach share the same sacred object — his on a leather cord, hers hidden in a costume. This is the visual motif for the film. When the loupe comes out of the pocket, she's stopped enduring and started acting.*

## The "ZZ" Stagnation
Like so many of their generation, she and Zach waited at a red light that never turned green. No specific dollar amount on the wall — the light was simply never green. Not stalled. Denied.
