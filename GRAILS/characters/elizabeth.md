# ELIZABETH (ZAB) — The Pragmatic Professional / VP of Authenticity

---

## Life Goal
Execute the perfect audit — use the Spectral Guard to prove that history has a physical, unassailable truth that even hype cannot rewrite.

## Theme Goal
To stop being a prestige asset and be a partner. To be valued for her expertise, not her packaging.

## The Vibe
Elizabeth is the "Adult in the Room" who has been turned into a "Prestige Asset." She is sophisticated and precise, but she carries the weariness of someone who traded her soul for a stability that turned out to be a gilded cage.

## Visual Arc — The Erasure of the Expert

**The Attic (with Zach):** Practical, durable curator-wear. Oversized denim shirts and heavy knits.

**The Transition:** Sharp, architectural designer suits. She finally feels like a "real" executive.

**The Event (with Togg):** "Costumed" in a WWE-style "Hot Businesswoman" uniform. Not a catsuit, but hyper-performative: a sharp, white structural blazer with exaggerated, almost comic-book shoulders, tailored to an extreme, integrated with glowing tech-accents that catch the 8K God-Ray light. A costume designed to make her look like a Character playing an Authenticator, rather than a woman doing her job.

## The ZZ Stagnation
Like so many of their generation, she and Zach were paralyzed by a low-grade, constant anxiety about the future. There was no specific dollar amount on the wall — they just never felt "safe" enough to cross the threshold into marriage and family. They waited for a green light from the universe that never stayed lit long enough for them to take the plunge.

## The Prestige Asset Realization
Togg dazzles her with the Good Life — Puerto Rican villas, private jets, Italian leather. He flirts with her in a way that feels like a partnership, until the moment she realizes she's just an Exotic Conquest. He doesn't want her advice; he wants the status of owning a woman who is both beautiful and brilliant — a collectible that isn't an influencer. She swallows her pride to save her career, until the heist forces her hand.

## The Tell
She keeps her original ZZ Heritage House loupe tucked into the pocket of her performative WWE blazer. The only thing on her person that carries sentimental value rather than market value.

She and Zach share the same sacred object — his on a leather cord around his neck, hers hidden inside a costume. This is the visual motif for the film.
