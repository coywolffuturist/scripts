# ELIZABETH (ZAB) — The Pragmatic Professional / VP of Authenticity

## The Vibe
Over the course of her trailblazing career authenticating sports memorabilia, Elizabeth has been the adult in the room, the one who actually ran the numbers, created new science, and pushed the industry forward. All too often she found herself cleaning up others' messes and forced to call it collaboration. For less determined wills, it would wear them down to dust. For Zab, it motivates her to break her career through to a level of self-sufficiency beyond the reach of idiots.

## Visual Arc — The Erasure of the Expert
Elizabeth runs two looks. In her AI-enhanced memoribilia authentication forensic lab it's as if she's back at ZZ's shop:&nbsp;vintage tees, hair in a paintbrush ponytail. Her second look is the power suit. Tailored, authoritative, every inch of it chosen to signal competence to rooms that need convincing, be it as an expert witness in a courtroom or with Togg on a livestream. But he keeps trying to doll her up like "Science Barbie", repackaging her for an audience that wants spectacle, not expertise. When she sees it boost her brand she begins high heeling her way down a slippery slope.

## The ZZ Stagnation
Like so many of their generation, the relationship between her and Zach was defined by a low-grade, paralyzing anxiety about the future. There was no specific dollar amount on the wall — they just never felt remotely "safe" enough to cross the threshold into marriage and family. They waited for a green light from the universe that never stayed lit long enough for them to take the plunge. She'll always love him, but the respect diminished until it was impossible for her to stay.

## The Prestige Asset Realization
Togg dazzles her with the Good Life — Puerto Rican villas, private jets, Italian leather. He flirts with her in a way that feels like a partnership, until the moment she realizes she's just an Exotic Conquest. He doesn't want her advice; he wants the status of owning a woman who is both beautiful and brilliant — a collectible that isn't an influencer. She swallows her pride to save her career, until the heist forces her hand.

## The Tell
She keeps her original ZZ Heritage House loupe tucked into the pocket of her performative WWE blazer. The only thing on her person that carries sentimental value rather than market value.

She and Zach share the same sacred object — his on a leather cord around his neck, hers hidden inside a costume. This is the visual motif for the film.

## Backstory
Elizabeth grew up as her father's daughter, a tomboy science nerd who could rattle off a reliever's career ERA because she stayed up coding her own sabermetrics (baseball stats) algos. After she started working at ZZs she and Zach became inseparable for the rest of of high school, passing notes in class that were half flirtation half sports factoid showdowns. The summer before senior year she took his virginity in ZZ's backroom restoration lab atop a Cal Ripken Jr. jersey.

After graduation, Elizabeth (nicknamed "Zab" by Zach) dove into&nbsp;computer science at UCLA, but still worked with Zach on the weekends at ZZ's. AI began to show real promise in the late 2010s, her career trajectory steepened fast — consulting work, research papers, early forensic tool builds. The gap between them widened quietly, without malice.
She kept waiting for him to catch the same updraft. He kept waiting for the universe to give them a green light. At some point she ran the numbers the way she ran every other audit: coldly, honestly. She couldn't afford to freeze her eggs. And even if she could — when she looked at the increasingly difficult world she'd be asking a child to inherit, she realized she needed a partner who was moving toward it, not away from it. Zach wasn't that man. Not yet. Maybe not ever. She'll always love him. But love without forward motion is just a beautiful artifact — authenticated, catalogued, and stored.

## Show Journey
Her arc moves from professional exile to radical self-reclamation. She entered Togg's world as a scientist and exits it as something harder to name — a woman who chose the truth over the career it cost her. The ZZ chapter closes not with resentment but with a quiet respect she couldn't afford to feel when she was still inside it.
