# Zach — The Earnest Sports Monk Turned Forger

---

## The Vibe
Genial, earnest, deeply kind — the man who still believes the truth is a shield. The Jimmy Stewart of the piece. The Good Shepherd of the hobby.

## Visual Fingerprint
Wears vintage, sun-faded sports tees — soft, paper-thin cotton from the 80s and 90s. Not hype vintage — shirts of defunct minor league teams or local high school championships. Carries a high-precision jeweler's loupe on a leather cord, used with the gentle reverence of a surgeon.

## Environmental Relationship (The Sanctuary)
His shop is a sensory time capsule. It smells of acid-free plastic, aged cardboard, and the faint, sweet ghost of 40-year-old bubblegum dust. He doesn't just "own" the shop; he curates a temple where the neighborhood feels safe from the clout-chasing world outside. The last store standing in a dying strip mall.

## The Flaw
His "Intellectual Arrogance" is actually an unshakable faith in the truth. He believes so deeply in the sanctity of history that he thinks the Truth is a shield. He is genial and patient, but he cannot comprehend that a man like Togg can simply "rewrite" reality if enough people are watching.

**The Key Line:** He thinks being Right is a superpower. He doesn't realize it's a target.

---

## Life Goal
Keep the shop alive. Buy enough time to prove that what he does — what he *is* — still matters in a world that's moved on.

## Theme Goal
To be seen. Not famous, not wealthy — *seen*. To have someone recognize that the truth he's devoted his life to protecting has real weight, real consequence. That sentiment isn't a losing bet.
