# GRAILS — Premise Document

---

## Logline
A humble sports memorabilia restorer fights for the soul of his craft when he's swept up into a Koreatown gang's heist of an influencer's multimillion dollar collection.

## Genre
Crime Thriller / Dark Comedy

## Tone
**Visual:** Sexy, noir, gritty — neon-soaked, claustrophobic, tactile. Uncut Gems palette. You can smell the acid-free plastic and the Koreatown karaoke bar in the same film.

**Emotional Register:** Ocean's Eleven warmth. The crew is charming, the mark deserves it, and you're rooting for the heist before you've decided if you should be. Anxious and complicit simultaneously.

## World
Contemporary Los Angeles. Three zones in collision:

- **The Valley** — sun-faded, analog, the old economy hanging on. Downstream victim of the Toggification of the world. Strip malls, sun-bleached everything, people who built their lives around things that used to matter.
- **Koreatown** — sovereign, invisible infrastructure, its own economy and rules. Invisible to both Zach and Togg.
- **The Hills / Arena / Event Space** — Togg's hyper-rendered Panopticon where everything is content and everyone is a collectible. 8K god-ray lighting, sentiment graphs, everything for sale.

The film moves between all three. The collision of their value systems is the engine.

**Theme:** The Toggification of the world impacts us all. Togg isn't just a villain — he's a symptom. The influencer economy didn't create him; it rewarded him. The Valley is living with the exhaust.

## Comps
*Uncut Gems* meets *Game Night* — with the soul of *Desk Set*

## Characters

### Zach — The Earnest Sports Monk Turned Forger
**Archetype:** The Good Shepherd of the hobby. The Jimmy Stewart of the piece.

**The Vibe:** Genial, earnest, deeply kind — the man who still believes the truth is a shield.

**Visual Fingerprint:** Wears vintage, sun-faded sports tees — soft, paper-thin cotton from the 80s and 90s. Not hype vintage — shirts of defunct minor league teams or local high school championships. Carries a high-precision jeweler's loupe on a leather cord, used with the gentle reverence of a surgeon.

**Environmental Relationship (The Sanctuary):** His shop is a sensory time capsule. It smells of acid-free plastic, aged cardboard, and the faint, sweet ghost of 40-year-old bubblegum dust. He doesn't just "own" the shop; he curates a temple where the neighborhood feels safe from the clout-chasing world outside.

**The Flaw (Tragic Naivety):** His "Intellectual Arrogance" is actually an unshakable faith in the truth. He believes so deeply in the sanctity of history that he thinks the Truth is a shield. He is genial and patient, but he cannot comprehend that a man like Togg can simply "rewrite" reality if enough people are watching.

**The Key Line:** He thinks being Right is a superpower. He doesn't realize it's a target.

---

### Gregg Togg — The Midas of Hype / The Predator of Value
**Archetype:** The Main Character of a world he purchased.

**The Vibe:** High-frequency, high-ego, hyper-saturated. He doesn't walk into a room; he "renders" into it. He believes "Authenticity" is just a marketing term and that enough "W" spam in a chat room can rewrite reality.

**Visual Fingerprint:** Over-Rendered. Bespoke Tech-Athleisure — matte black, high-contrast fabrics with his gold "GT" sigil. Skin has a professional-grade, unnaturally smooth glow. Movements choreographed for the 8K God-Ray lighting that follows him. A walking YouTube thumbnail.

**The "Prestige Asset" Philosophy:** To Togg, everything — and everyone — is a collectible. He doesn't cherish artifacts; he cherishes the clout they project. He views Elizabeth as an Exotic Conquest — a high-IQ, high-beauty asset that validates him as a "Serious Person." A "Mint 10" card: a prestige item to be envied, not a partner to be heard.

**Environmental Relationship (The Panopticon):** Treats every space as his private Watch-to-Earn theater. Doesn't look at people; looks at the Sentiment Graph on his smart-lens. If the graph isn't going up, he changes the narrative by any means necessary.

---

### Elizabeth (Zab) — The Pragmatic Professional / VP of Authenticity
**Archetype:** The Adult in the Room who has been turned into a Prestige Asset.

**The Vibe:** Sophisticated and precise, carrying the weariness of someone who traded her soul for a stability that turned out to be a gilded cage.

**Visual Arc (The Erasure of the Expert):**
- *The Attic (with Zach):* Practical, durable curator-wear. Oversized denim shirts and heavy knits.
- *The Transition:* Sharp, architectural designer suits. She finally feels like a "real" executive.
- *The Event (with Togg):* Costumed in a WWE-style "Hot Businesswoman" uniform. A sharp, white structural blazer with exaggerated comic-book shoulders, integrated with glowing tech-accents that catch the 8K God-Ray light. A costume designed to make her look like a Character playing an Authenticator, rather than a woman doing her job.

**The "ZZ" Stagnation:** Like so many of their generation, she and Zach were paralyzed by low-grade, constant anxiety about the future. No specific dollar amount on the wall — they just never felt "safe" enough to cross the threshold into marriage and family. Waiting for a green light from the universe that never stayed lit long enough.

**The "Prestige Asset" Realization:** Togg dazzles her with the Good Life — Puerto Rican villas, private jets, Italian leather. He flirts with her in a way that feels like a partnership, until she realizes she's just an Exotic Conquest. She swallows her pride to save her career, until the heist forces her hand.

**The Tell:** She keeps her original ZZ Heritage House loupe tucked into the pocket of her performative WWE blazer. The only thing on her person that carries sentimental value rather than market value.

*Note: She and Zach share the same sacred object — his on a leather cord, hers hidden in a costume. This is the visual motif for the film.*

---

### Jae-Wook — The Lieutenant
**Archetype:** The operator with a trap in his name. ("Talented" + "hook/snare")

**The Vibe:** Noble criminal. Never hurts anyone who doesn't deserve it. Only steals from collectors who don't care about the actual item and its lore. But he is a thief, a criminal, and associates with genuinely bad people.

**Role:** Lieutenant in a local Koreatown Korean gang. Mastermind of the heist. Hired Zach to create the replica without revealing its true purpose.

---

### Hye-Jin — The Obvious Suspect
**Name meaning:** "Bright/wise" + "precious/rare." An expert hidden inside a pretty package.

**Role:** One of two doumis planted inside Togg's retinue. Met Togg at a Koreatown karaoke party. Togg assumes she's a ditz; she's clocking his every move.

**The Misdirect:** Zach, when he gets suspicious, assumes Hye-Jin is Jae-Wook's inside woman. She is not.

---

### Deja — The One Who Was Right There
**Role:** The other doumi. The actual inside woman. Black. The one nobody suspects.

**The Pleasure:** She's been the most present — laughing at Togg's jokes, charming everyone — while running the full intelligence operation. The reveal lands harder because she was hiding in plain sight.

---

## Structure Notes

**Act 1→2 Turn:** Zach watches Elizabeth testify as an expert witness in a trial of counterfeit signature baseballs. He notes the ethnicity of the defendant (Korean) and the person who hired him (Korean) and gets suspicious. A quiet, cold realization — not a dramatic confrontation.

**The MacGuffin:** 1986 Fleer unopened box set + Michael Jordan RC + Kobe Bryant RC. Legendary items. The collector world already treats them like holy relics.

**The Exploit:** Togg's prestige philosophy — his inability to see past surface value — is exactly what makes him blind to the doumis. His own worldview is his vulnerability.
