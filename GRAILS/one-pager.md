# GRAILS — One-Pager
*Zen Story Architect × Wroom Pipeline*

---

## 1 + 2. Audience & Genre

GRAILS is a Heist Thriller / Dark Comedy for the audience that made *Uncut Gems* a cultural obsession, turned *Game Night* into a sleeper phenomenon, and keeps *Knives Out* sequels in theaters every two years. They've never seen a caper built inside the world of multi-million dollar sports memorabilia collectibles — until now.

---

## 3. Antecedents

*Uncut Gems* meets *Game Night* in the world of multi-million dollar sports memorabilia collectibles.

---

## 4. The "What If"

What if your greatest skill became the key to committing a crime you couldn't get out of?

---

## 5. Logline

A humble sports memorabilia restorer fights for the soul of his craft when he's swept up into a Koreatown gang's heist of an influencer's multimillion-dollar collection.

---

## 6. Protagonist

Zach is a meticulous sports memorabilia restorer — the last true believer in a dying strip mall shop, a man whose entire identity is built around the sanctity of authentic things. His devotion to the truth is what's killing his business. It's also what makes his craftsmanship the most valuable weapon in a multi-million dollar caper.

---

## 7. Call to Adventure

Over soju on a Koreatown rooftop, a charming criminal named Jae-Wook offers Zach $100,000 to make the greatest restoration of his life — no questions asked, no accountability attached. For a man with two months of rent left, the answer is obvious. The problem is what he'll have to become to earn it.

---

## 8. Desire

Zach wants to save his shop and honor his father — but forging the greatest sports collectible in history reveals something he never knew about himself: he's been playing by rules that nobody else follows anymore, and he's a natural at breaking them.

---

## 9. Conflict

Zach has never so much as jaywalked. Now he's forging a million-dollar collectible for a Koreatown gang, reverse-engineering his ex-fiancée's AI authentication system, and helping defraud the most famous sports influencer in the world — and somehow, terrifyingly, he's getting good at it.

---

## 10. Action

Zach throws himself into the job with the same obsessive precision he's applied to every restoration in his career — procuring materials, reverse-engineering Elizabeth's AI, attending Saudi sit-downs he has no business being in — discovering that the skills that couldn't save his shop might just pull off the impossible.

---

## 11. The Major Setback

In a single week, Zach loses everything — his father's shop shuttered, the family collection liquidated, and Elizabeth officially on Togg's arm in a PR move designed to make the world forget her credibility is under question. He's already past the point of no return. Now he has nothing left to go back to.

---

## 12. The Climax

On a live stream watched by millions, Zach's forgery passes Elizabeth's AI authentication — and when Togg cracks open the box and the Jordan rookie drops, the prediction markets resolve and $GOAT goes stratospheric. Then Elizabeth sees something only she would recognize. The crew has already shorted everything. The crash is instantaneous, public, and total.

---

## 13. Deeper Meaning

The influencer economy didn't happen to us. We funded it, followed it, and let it set the price on things we said we loved. *Grails* asks what it costs to put our hearts back into the loves technology hollowed out.
